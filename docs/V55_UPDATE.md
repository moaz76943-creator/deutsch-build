# V55 — Verb-first quality update

- Added 123 new unique vocabulary records (38 new verbs + 85 additional words) after a strict no-duplicate check against the existing 7,013 headwords.
- Focused on verb learning: new regular, irregular and separable verbs are grouped by function and each has six Präsens forms, six German examples and Arabic translations.
- Corrected obvious legacy conjugation errors, including `wohnen`, `zustimmen`, `ablehnen` and `übersetzen`.
- Corrected obvious accusative examples such as `Ich mag den Film.` and `Ich kaufe den Käse.`.

A 500-word expansion was intentionally not fabricated: the quality gate rejected the available candidate pool rather than inserting duplicate or synthetic compounds.
