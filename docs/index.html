Privacy Policy

Global Language Learner

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Global Language Learner is designed as an offline-first language-learning application. Learning records are stored locally on the user's device and are not sent to developer-controlled servers.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Global Language Learner (the "App") accesses, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the current Android release. It also explains the permissions used by the App and how you can remove locally stored learning data.

2. Data collection and sharing

The App does not require you to create a personal or cloud account to use its core learning features. The current Android release does not send your learning records to developer-controlled servers.

The App does not use developer-controlled servers to collect or share:

  • your vocabulary or saved learning items;
  • your learning progress or review history;
  • your SRS review state;
  • audio recordings from pronunciation exercises; or
  • personal profile information for the core learning experience.

3. Local storage and SQLite

Learning data is stored locally on your device. The App uses local SQLite databases and other local application storage where appropriate to maintain vocabulary, progress, review state, Spaced Repetition System (SRS) scheduling, and related learning settings.

This data remains under the control of the device and is not synchronized to a developer-controlled cloud account.

4. Microphone and pronunciation exercises

The App requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO permission only for pronunciation and speech-practice features that you choose to use.

The App itself does not save microphone recordings and does not upload audio recordings to developer-controlled servers.

Speech recognition is requested through the speech-recognition service configured on your device. Depending on your device, operating system, language pack, and configured speech service, recognition may be performed on-device or may be handled according to the policies of that speech-service provider. You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in Android settings; doing so only affects features that require microphone input.

5. Notifications

The App may request the Android POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission to provide optional local reminders for learning and review sessions. These notifications are not required for the core learning features.

You can disable notifications at any time through the App where controls are available or through your device's Android notification settings.

6. Accounts

No personal or cloud account is required to use the App's core learning features. Because the current core experience does not create a developer-hosted user account, there is no cloud learning profile to delete from developer servers.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your device while the App and its local data are present. You can remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

Because the App does not maintain a developer-controlled copy of your local learning records, the developer cannot restore those local records after they have been deleted from your device.

8. Data security

The App follows a local-first design intended to minimize unnecessary data transmission. The current Android release does not request the Android INTERNET permission, and learning records are stored within the App's local storage boundary.

Device security also depends on your operating system, device configuration, access controls, and other software installed on your device.

9. Third-party services

The App does not use developer-controlled external servers to collect your learning records. Some device capabilities, such as speech recognition, may be provided by your operating system or a speech-service provider installed or configured on your device. Those services may be governed by their own privacy terms.

10. Children's privacy

The App does not require a personal account for its core learning features and does not use developer-controlled servers to collect learning records. If the App's audience, features, or data practices change in a way that creates additional legal obligations for children, this policy and the relevant disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, permissions, or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.
