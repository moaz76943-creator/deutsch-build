# V53 Vocabulary & Verb Quality Update

- Added 200 additional non-duplicate German vocabulary entries, bringing the dataset to 7,013 unique headwords.
- New vocabulary is separated into practical categories such as home, transport, shopping, work, study, travel, health, technology, community, and weather/nature.
- Every added item includes an Arabic translation and an Arabic translation of its German example. Nouns include article and plural where applicable.
- Corrected obvious legacy content issues found during this pass, including an incomplete example for **nur**, a malformed Chefzimmer entry, and the Arabic examples for **werden**.
- Verb area remains divided into 11 sections. Each verb has six Präsens forms, an example for each form, and Arabic translation for the verb and every example.
